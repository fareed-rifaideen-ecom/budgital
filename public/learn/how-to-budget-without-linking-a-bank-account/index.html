Privacy-first budgeting guide

How to budget without linking a bank account

Short answer: use a small category plan, enter transactions manually or import a CSV, and keep a backup file so your budget is portable without sharing bank credentials.

Why choose a bank-free workflow?

Some people want to avoid account aggregation, third-party credentials, regional institution coverage, or a service that stores their financial history. A local workflow trades some automation for direct control and a smaller data boundary.

A simple five-step system

  1. Choose a monthly planning period and currency.
  2. Create only the categories you actually use.
  3. Enter income and expenses as they happen, or import a CSV on a weekly rhythm.
  4. Compare planned and actual category totals.
  5. Export a backup before clearing browser data or switching devices.

What you give up

You do not get automatic bank syncing, real-time balance refreshes, or subscription detection unless you build a separate workflow. The payoff is that you can see exactly what is stored, which entries were imported, and when anything is shared.

Can AI still be private?

AI can be optional and scoped. A safer design sends aggregate month totals and category totals to a server-side model, not raw descriptions or individual transactions. The app should explain the payload before the user asks for help and preserve deterministic local insights when AI is off.
