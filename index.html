A calmer way to budget

Free private budget app for monthly spending

Budgetly helps you log income and expenses, plan a monthly amount for each category, and see what is left. It works in your browser without a bank connection or account, so you can start with manual entries or import a CSV.

What makes Budgetly different?

Budgetly is local-first: your working budget stays in this browser unless you export it or choose to send aggregate totals to the optional Gemini insight feature.

What can I track?

Track income, expenses, categories, monthly budgets, spending by category, and month-over-month changes. Use the dashboard for plain-English signals instead of a spreadsheet full of formulas.

Can I use AI privately?

Yes, optionally. Budgetly sends only month totals and category totals to its server-side Gemini proxy. Individual transactions and descriptions remain local.
